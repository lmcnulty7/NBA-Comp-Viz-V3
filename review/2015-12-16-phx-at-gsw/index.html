Notes on this game

  • Held-out game: the frozen models never saw it, so every prediction shown here is out of sample.
  • Positions: 5 frames a second, on the grid of the stored ghost rows (every fifth frame from the possession's first frame). t is frame time at 25 frames a second, so it keeps running while the game clock is stopped. n_frames counts the frames of the possession file. The last frame is the last grid frame at or before the possession's end, and duration_s is its t, so a shot time or an EPV time can lie up to 0.16 s after the last frame.
  • Attack direction: the tracking data does not say which rim a team attacks. It is inferred per team and period by a majority vote of where the offense stands. The ball's side over the last third of a possession agrees with it in 212 of 215 possessions of at least two seconds.
  • xFG: the frozen model's probability that the shot goes in, a point estimate (the lane stores no interval for one shot). xfg in this list is the last field-goal attempt of the possession. xfg_bucket is its fifth among the 2939 held-out attempts with a prediction, from 18 held-out games (edges in xfg_bucket_edges). 181 of those attempts belong to 1 held-out game(s) with no possessions table (01.23.2016.CHI.at.CLE), so the review cannot show them. The other 2758 are in the games shown here.
  • EPV: expected points for the offense to the end of the possession, the model's raw output, about one value a second of game clock, never clipped. Each value is a point estimate.
  • Ghost defenders show an average defense: where an average defender would be 1 s after his real position, given how the ball and the attackers really moved. Each of the five is computed alone, with the other four real, and is a point estimate. A point more than 10 ft outside the court is not drawn.
  • Shots: 179 attempts of this game have a prediction (0 of them fall in no possession and are not shown). 107 have a located release (time and place from tracking). 72 take their time from the play-by-play frame, which marks the logged outcome. 72 take their place from the shot chart. 125 had their contest features measured by the xFG lane; for the others the prediction rests on location, period, clock and the shooter prior. 0 attempts have no prediction.
  • Shot chart orientation: checked on 57 shots of this game that have a located release and lie at least 5 ft off the middle of the court. 55 of them sit nearer the shooter's tracked position when the chart's sideline axis runs against the tracking y axis. A chart marker sits a median of 3.4 ft from the tracked release position (104 shots), so a chart location is approximate. It is used only where no release was located.
  • Shooter: the play-by-play name matched to a tracked player; where that match failed, the shot chart's player (13 shots of this game).
  • This game: 216 possessions, 0 with a single frame, 2 with a clock gap, 17 without ghost defenders.
  • Featured possessions (PLAN.md D48): one drawn at random (seed 0) from each of the 10 cells of result (made, missed) by xFG fifth, among possessions that end in a field-goal attempt with a located release, an xFG, at least 5 EPV states, ghosts on at least 80% of the frames, no clock gap, 10 tracked players and 6 to 24 s of play. 10 of 10 cells have a pick.
