# P8-E2: shots and rebounds from the play-by-play and the clock

Branch r-p8-events, written 2026-11-21 under PLAN.md D83 to D88, D97 to D100 and D128 to D137, and
docs/research/P8_FEASIBILITY.md sections 6.2 and 8 (experiment E2, ranked first). No training, no GPU, no new
truth rule, no change to the pinned scorer (tools/p8_scorecard.py, D88).

## 1. Pre-registration (written and committed before any E2 number)

### 1.1 What was seen before this was written

While reading file formats: the first 50 or so per-window clock offsets of `labels/gsw_bkn_2015_offsets.json` in
the P8 workspace (values from -1.5 to +1.5 s), the first spans of three section time maps, the shot and rebound
truth counts that the existing r23 scorecards print (gsw_bkn_2015: 112 shots and 61 rebounds near live wide
frames; gsw_ind_2016: 139 and 85), and the head of one frozen shot_frames and one rebound_frames table. No
placement was made and no found share was computed.

### 1.2 Method (what E2 predicts)

For every play-by-play field-goal attempt (`kind` shot_made or shot_missed) and every rebound (`kind` rebound) of
a development game, at (period, clock_s):

- **M0 (primary).** Place the event at the video frame whose OCR clock equals the play-by-play clock. The time map
  is sportvu/local_sync.py's, as written: running spans of the 1 Hz scorebug reads, the clock interpolated
  between the first and last read of a span. An event is placed in a span when the span has the same period and
  `c1 - 0.5 <= clock_s <= c0 + 0.5` (0.5 s is the scorecard's `event_live_wide_s`); the frame is the time map
  frame of that span with the nearest clock. Nothing is fitted.
- **M1 (secondary, labelled calibrated).** The same with one constant lag L per scored game: the event is placed
  where the OCR clock equals `clock_s - L`, L being the median, over windows, of the R1.3 Colab camera-truth
  clock offsets (`offset_s` in the main checkout's `data/sportvu/labels/<game>/*.jsonl`) of the other R1.3 games
  (leave one game out). No offset of the scored game enters its own L.
- **Chain rule (both).** OCR misreads make running spans whose clock is out of order (a span reading 1st 1:18
  among spans near 11:00). Before placing, the spans of a game, ordered by section and frame, are reduced to the
  chain of largest total video seconds in which game time never runs backward: span b may follow span a when b's
  period is later, or the period is the same and `c0(b) <= c1(a) + 1.0` (1.0 s is local_sync's `RUN_TOL_S`).
  Spans off the chain place nothing. This uses the video's own reads only, never SportVU.

The written prediction for the scorer is `<window>_events.json` (`{"events": [{"frame", "kind"}]}`) in a new
build folder `builds/e2` of the P8 workspace, one file per label window whose section span is on the chain. The
build folder links the V3 label build's sidecars (`_frames.json`, `_identity.json`) so the scorer has a sidecar
per window; the frozen rows of build e2 are therefore V3's rows and are not an E2 result.

### 1.3 Truth and matching rule

The contract play-by-play's `frame` is the SportVU frame of the same period whose game clock is nearest the
play-by-play clock (docs/DATA_CONTRACT.md, within 2 s). In game time it is therefore the play-by-play clock
itself, so comparing a placement with it in game time would be circular. What can be measured is where that
SportVU moment sits in the video, through a sync that does not come from the play-by-play:

- **Row A, the verdict (scorecard v2 as pinned, truth `camera`).** The scorer's own event rows: the truth is the
  play-by-play event on SportVU's clock, placed on the window's video through the window's camera-truth clock
  offset (true clock = OCR clock + offset, solved at the camera fit by matching SportVU players to boxes,
  `labels/<game>_offsets.json`). Truth events are those within 0.5 s of a live wide frame of the window. A
  placement's true clock is the OCR clock of its frame plus the same offset, kept when it is within 0.5 s of a
  live wide frame. **Found within 1.0 s** means `tools.p8_scorecard.match_events`: one to one, nearest first,
  same period, |true clock of placement - SportVU clock of event| <= 1.0 s. **False events** are kept
  placements matched to no truth event, per true event. Computed by the pinned `score_window` and `v2_rows`,
  unchanged, and written as the pinned scorecard file `scorecards/<game>__e2__camera.json`.
- **Row B, sensitivity (truth `b4`).** The same rows with the frozen B4 truth's per-window offset
  (sportvu.truth.window_truth), on every game whose V3 label build exists. It shows how much the verdict depends
  on the offset solve, which D99 found fragile (0.3 to 0.8 s moves between builds).
- **Row C, diagnostic (physical SportVU frames).** Section 6.2 names the release frame and the moment the ball is
  secured. The frozen SportVU tables locate them from the ball: `release_frame` of analytics/shot_frames.py and
  `rebound_frame` of analytics/rebound_frames.py (stored under DATA_ROOT/analytics, training games only). For
  every placed event that has such a frame (>= 0): the game-time part `clock_s - game_clock(physical frame)`,
  found when |.| <= 1.0 s; and, on windows with a camera-truth offset, the full error
  `clock_s + offset - game_clock(physical frame)`. The frozen tables cover only part of the events (shooter name
  joins, no release found; D94), so their coverage is printed beside the row. Row C reads no verdict.

### 1.4 Targets (section 6.2 and the pinned constants, copied)

- Shots: at least 95 percent of field-goal attempts found within 1.0 s (`shots_found_target` 0.95,
  `event_tol_s` 1.0) and at most 5 percent false events per true event (`shots_false_max` 0.05).
- Rebounds: at least 90 percent found within 1.0 s (`rebounds_found_target` 0.90).
- An event counts when a live wide frame of its period lies within 0.5 s of it (`event_live_wide_s` 0.5).
- Pass is judged on the point estimate of row A; the intervals are printed beside it.

### 1.5 Coverage and gaps (all games with time maps)

On the chained section spans, every event is either placed or falls in a gap of one class:

- **period not mapped**: no chained span in its period;
- **between sections**: the chained spans before and after it (in game time) lie in different sections;
- **video skips game time**: same section, and the video between the two spans is shorter than the game time
  between them by more than 1.0 s (an edit or a cut in the upload);
- **clock ran unread**: same section, the clock ran while the scorebug was not read as running. The time map keeps
  no raw reads, so a replay during live play, a cut away to a close-up or crowd without the scorebug, a graphic
  over the bug and an OCR miss cannot be told apart; they are one class.

Dead balls (the clock stopped between two spans, game time gap <= 1.0 s) hold no field-goal attempt; their video
seconds are reported. The share of placed events, events per class, and the share of placed events that reach
the scorer's universe (a label window, near a live wide frame) are printed per game.

### 1.6 Intervals

- Window bootstrap: cluster bootstrap over the scorecard's label windows (rows A and B) or over chained section
  spans (rows C and coverage), 1000 resamples, seed 0, 95 percent percentile interval
  (`tools.p8_scorecard.boot_windows`, the scorer's own).
- Game bootstrap: the same over games, where at least 2 pooled games carry the row. With the camera truth on one
  pooled game only, row A has no game bootstrap; that is said, not hidden.

### 1.7 Games

Development games of `tools.p8_common.dev_games()` that have section time maps in the P8 workspace:
gsw_bkn_2015, gsw_cha_2016, gsw_sac_2015 (R1.3) and nyk_bos_2016 (channel) are pooled; gsw_ind_2016 is reported
apart and kept out of every pooled row (D98). okc_sac_2015 and por_okc_2016 have no time map and are listed as
not run. The camera truth exists for gsw_bkn_2015 and gsw_ind_2016 only (P8 night 2 is held, D99 and D100), so
row A pools one game. Every tag, SportVU id and video id passes `tools.p8_common.guard` before any file is read.

### 1.8 Fixed before any number

The chain rule, the 0.5 s placement margin, the gap classes, M1's leave-one-game-out median and every tolerance
above are fixed here. Nothing is changed after a result is seen; any later idea is reported as post hoc.
