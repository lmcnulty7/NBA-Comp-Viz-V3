# What can I do with this?

Review a possession. Choose from {manifest.n_possessions} possessions. They come from {manifest.n_games} games that were kept out of model training. See shot quality, possession value over time, and where an average defense would have stood.

Prepare for a game. Look up a player: shot making, the pull on defenders, rebounding, the risk of missing the next game, and similar players.

See the results. Every model with its test numbers. The ones that failed are listed too.

## Notes

- Open the review on a featured possession and play it: shot quality at the release, possession value over time, the average-defense markers.
- On the results page, go to a failed row first. The decision-quality model failed its check, so it is shown as tested with no signal. Read its held-out value and its interval from the row.
- Every model number on these pages comes from sensor data or play-by-play. The broadcast pipeline is behind only the demo and the tracking scorecard.
