Where every player stood, and what it was worth.

Player tracking recovered from broadcast video, validated against the one public half-season of league tracking data, and an analytics layer (shot quality, rebounds, possession value) built on that data with leakage control first. Eleven models were frozen and then scored once on games they never saw.

The binding constraint

Public frame-level tracking exists for one half-season.

SportVU logs with all ten players and the ball at 25 Hz are public only for 2015-10-27 to 2016-01-23. Every model that needs positions trains there. Later seasons are aggregates and serve as validation targets. Eighteen games are frozen as held out: a scorecard game and every game at one arena, so generalisation to an unseen building is measured, never tuned.

Results

Frozen first, then scored once on unseen games.

Each model was chosen on 604 training games (or four training seasons) by a rule fixed before it ran, frozen at a tagged commit, and only then scored on the held-out games: 17 or 18 games for the tracking models, the whole 2024-25 season for the two play-by-play models. Intervals are 95 percent bootstraps over games; a held-out value without one is a point estimate over 17 or 18 games.

Roadmap

Six phases, each closed by a measured gate.

Baselines before deep models, one evaluation protocol for all of them: game-level splits, bootstrap intervals over games, calibration reported next to accuracy, a 300-sample floor before any ranking.

  1. 0
    Data foundation: release and rebound frames, possessions, evaluation harness, held-out guard. built
    gate: release frame within 0.4 s on at least 90% of hand-audited high-confidence shots. 96.7% (323 of 334, met)
  2. 1
    xFG and rebound probability: defender distance and closing speed at release; multinomial over the players at the rim. held out scored
    gate: both beat their baselines. xFG 0.6232 against the location baseline's 0.6735; rebounds 1.71 against the uniform 2.39
  3. 2
    EPV, gravity, matchups: one-second states, gradient boosting with isotonic calibration, then a GRU that beat it. held out scored
    gate: calibration slope 0.9 to 1.1 on held-out games. EPV 1.02 overall, 1.23 in the first 4 seconds (missed there)
  4. 3
    Stints, win probability, lineups, embeddings on five seasons of play-by-play. held out scored
    gate: win probability calibrated on a held-out season. Slopes 0.95 to 1.00, ECE at most 0.021 (met)
  5. 4
    Hand labels, ball-screen detection, ghosting: the one deep model, after a linear baseline. ghosting done labels pending
    gate: ghosting beats linear (3.66 ft against 4.54, met); screen F1 over 0.8 waits for the labels
  6. 5
    Coverage recognition, decision quality. decisions failed coverage pending
    gate: reported. Decision quality shows no validated signal; coverage waits for the labels
Assumptions and provenance
  • Updated unattended on 2026-11-12 from reports/results.md: the stage 8 run of the models frozen at tag freeze-v1, each scored on its held-out games exactly once. Every number is copied from a lane report; the held-out games are every game at one arena plus one scorecard game.
  • The vision-track error is the 2026-10-03 measurement on the held-out game, before a camera-model fix whose adoption is pending; it is shown because it is the last number on record, not because it is current.
  • The possession animation uses synthetic coordinates and illustrates the contract rules only.
  • Raw tracking logs are league property and stay private; the repository publishes code, schemas, aggregates and reports.
  • No NBA marks, logos or player likenesses are used. Team and arena names appear only as data identifiers.
