Model status works; beats the location-only baseline by 0.050 on held-out; held-out calibration worse (ECE 0.019)

  • Makes above expected per 100 attempts. Expected is the lane's location and contest model (contest_logistic), which does not know who is shooting. It is a candidate the lane did not select.
  • That model has a structured error of its own: its out-of-fold calibration table is given in shot_making_baseline. baseline_error_per_100 is the part of a player's value that this table explains for his mix of shots: his attempts in each calibration bin times that bin's observed minus predicted rate, per 100 attempts. Read the two numbers together.
  • frozen_model_residual_per_100 is the residual of the frozen xFG model. That model includes a shooter prior: it already knows the shooter, so its residual is not a measure over expected. It is given for reference only.
  • Attempts left out: 5970 of 101853 out-of-fold attempts, which come from 607 training games (more than the tracked training games, because a few training games have shot-chart rows and no tracking tables). An attempt counts for a player only when the tracked attempt names him as the shooter resolved from the play-by-play. Of those left out, 493 belong to 3 training game(s) with no shot_frames table, so there is no tracked attempt to join; 5477 have a tracked attempt whose shooter the play-by-play name match did not resolve. Of the attempts whose shooter the name match did not resolve, the xFG lane gave 5079 to the player nearest the ball at release, for its shooter prior and contest features, and 398 have no shooter in it and no contest features.
  • A value is withheld, whatever its sample, when the play-by-play name fits the player and a teammate alike, or when attempts whose shooter the name match did not resolve fit him and would lift him to the minimum sample. The player page says which.
  • In the training games, 611 tracked attempts carry a play-by-play name that fits two teammates; the lane gave each of them to the first of the two.
  • fg_pct is descriptive: the share of the player's counted attempts that went in.
  • Interval: 95% percentile bootstrap over the player's own games (200 resamples, seed 0). It belongs to over_expected_per_100. fg_pct, expected_fg_pct, baseline_error_per_100 and frozen_model_residual_per_100 are point estimates: no interval was computed for them.

Rebounding

Not shown. 100 chances is below the minimum of 300.

Model status works, far above the 2.39 floor; held-out worse by 0.11

  • The rate is descriptive: rebounds per 100 chances in training games. A chance is a missed shot with a located rim frame while the player was on the floor, so a total here is not a box-score total.
  • The model expectation is the selected model's out-of-fold probability. It already uses the player's rebounding rate in other games, so the gap between the two is not a skill number.
  • The lane dropped 1032 misses because it could not match the play-by-play rebounder to a player. A rate is withheld, with its model expectation, when those misses could move it by 0.5 per 100 chances or more either way: down for the misses that may be his, up for the misses he was on the floor for, each a chance he did not get. The player page says which. Who was on the floor comes from the tracking frames at the rim frame for 1032 of those misses.
  • Interval: 95% percentile bootstrap over the player's own games (200 resamples, seed 0). It belongs to rate_per_100. model_expected_per_100 is a point estimate: no interval was computed for it.
